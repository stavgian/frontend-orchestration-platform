One page, built from independent apps

A catalog, a cart and a checkout, each built with a different technology and deployed on its own. The shell portal reads a manifest, loads them at runtime, and gives them one shared message channel. That channel is the only way they work together.

  1. Events “Something happened”: the catalog announces a product; the cart announces its new total.
  2. Requests “I need an answer”: checkout confirms the total with the cart before ordering.
  3. Presence “Who's here?”: checkout notices when the cart comes online and catches up.
