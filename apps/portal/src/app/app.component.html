One page, built from independent apps

A catalog, a cart and a checkout, each built with a different technology and deployed on its own. The portal loads them at runtime; they work together only by talking over one shared message channel.

  1. Events “Something happened”: the catalog announces a product was added.
  2. Requests “I need an answer”: checkout asks the cart for its total.
  3. Presence “Who's here?”: every app knows who is connected.
