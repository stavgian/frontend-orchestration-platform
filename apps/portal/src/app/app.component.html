Frontend Orchestration Platform · Live demo

Orchestrate micro‑frontends at runtime.

Any framework, deployed on its own, discovered from a manifest, loaded on demand and connected through one message channel. This demo runs four of them: Angular, React, Vue and plain JavaScript.

  1. Events “Something happened.” The sender does not know who is listening. Add a product: the cart, checkout and shipping all react.
  2. Direct messages “This is for you.” The same message, addressed to a single app. “Checkout →” in the cart reaches checkout only. The catalog never sees it.
  3. Requests “I need an answer.” One app asks, and the app that knows replies. On “Place order”, checkout asks the cart to confirm the total.
  4. Presence “Who is here?” Apps announce when they join and when they leave. Checkout waits for the cart, and “Ping all apps” asks everyone at once.
